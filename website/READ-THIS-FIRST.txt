BEFORE YOU UPLOAD: ONE THING YOU MUST DO
----------------------------------------
There are 36 placeholders across the site that read:

    [[REGISTRATION-NUMBER]]
    [[REGISTERING-COUNCIL]]

I have not invented a registration number, and I will not. Find and replace
both placeholders throughout every file before this goes live:

    [[REGISTRATION-NUMBER]]  ->  your medical register number

                                 for example: Travancore-Cochin Medical Council

These appear in the homepage footer, the three policy pages, the eight
procedure pages, and the structured data. If you upload without replacing
them, visitors will see the raw placeholder text.

Before filling them in, please also confirm that every qualification shown on
the site is registered with that council, because displaying an unregistered
additional qualification is a separate problem.

NOTE ON THE REMOVED "ABOUT THIS INFORMATION" BLOCK
---------------------------------------------------
You asked for this block to be removed and it has been. Two consequences
worth knowing, so the decision is an informed one:

  1. Paragraph 7.2 of the guidelines requires artificial-intelligence
     generated promotional content to carry a source mark stating that the
     origin of the content is artificial intelligence. Whether patient
     education pages on a practice website count as promotional is
     genuinely arguable, and that argument is on the list for your lawyer.
     Without the block, the site makes no such disclosure.

  2. Your own content rules call for an author profile, a last reviewed
     date and references on every medical page. The block was also the
     clearest evidence that the pages are educational rather than
     promotional, which is useful if that distinction is ever tested.

On 11 October you also asked for the "Content preparation" lines to come off
the three policy pages, and the "How this content is prepared" section on the
medical disclaimer page. Those are gone too. The site now carries no
artificial-intelligence disclosure anywhere.

The registration number and council still sit in the footer of every page, so
Explanation III is satisfied, and the policy pages keep their last-reviewed
dates.

If you removed the block because it was visually bulky rather than because
you disagreed with its content, a single discreet line under each page
heading would serve the same purpose. Say the word and I will add it.

WHAT CANNOT BE FIXED BY REMOVING CONTENT
----------------------------------------
Three items are positive obligations or matters of judgement. Deleting
content does not satisfy them:

  1. Explanation III requires your name, qualifications, registration status
     and register number to be disclosed. This needs your number, not an
     edit. 34 placeholders remain.
  2. Paragraph 7.2 requires an artificial-intelligence source mark on
     AI-generated promotional content. You asked for that disclosure to be
     taken off, so the site now has none.
  3. Paragraph 7.6 brings in the data protection legislation. The privacy
     page describes your real data flows, but the legal framing needs a
     lawyer.

Also outside the website: Instagram posts need the Explanation III
disclosure, and patient reviews must not be requested.
