Medical information disclaimer

The health information on this website is general. It is not a diagnosis and does not replace a consultation.

This website is general information, not medical advice

The health information published here describes conditions, investigations and treatments in general terms. It is written for patients, and it is not advice about your own case.

Your symptoms, your examination findings and your medical history together determine what is wrong and what should be done about it. No website can substitute for that. Please do not start, stop or change any treatment on the basis of something you read here.

Get urgent medical help for these

Do not use the appointment form, WhatsApp or email if you have any of the following. Go to the nearest emergency department or call 108.

  • Difficulty breathing, noisy laboured breathing, or a swallowed or inserted foreign body
  • Heavy bleeding from the nose, mouth or ear that does not stop
  • Sudden hearing loss in one or both ears — this is time-critical
  • Severe headache of sudden onset, or dizziness with slurred speech, double vision, or weakness or numbness of the face or limbs
  • A swollen, painful neck with high fever, or inability to swallow your own saliva
  • Severe facial swelling, or swelling around the eye with disturbed vision

What results can and cannot tell you

The symptom questionnaires on this website are validated instruments used in ear, nose and throat practice to record how symptoms affect a person. They measure symptom burden. They do not make a diagnosis, they do not establish a cause, and a score on its own does not mean any particular treatment is needed.

A score is useful as a starting point for a consultation and as a way of tracking change over time. It should be interpreted by a doctor alongside your history and examination.

Treatment outcomes vary

Where recovery times, typical results or figures from published studies are mentioned on this website, they describe what has been reported in general. They are not a prediction for you and not a promise. What you can expect in your own case is discussed with you before any treatment is agreed, including the alternatives and the risks.
