# 1. Google Business Profile (GBP) – Audit & Optimisation

> GBP is the single biggest driver of "near me" and Google Maps visibility. Google ranks local results on **relevance, distance and prominence**. You can't change distance. Relevance comes from a complete, accurate profile. Prominence comes from reviews, links, citations and activity.

### Business-name rule (important)
Do **not** add keywords to the name (for example "Dr. Bibin Baby – Best ENT Specialist Pala Vertigo Clinic"). This breaks Google's guidelines, can get the profile suspended, and goes against NMC's rules on self-praise. Use the real name on your signboard.

### Two locations: what to do
- **Pala clinic** (your own practice): one GBP, named after the practice.
- **Sunrise Hospital, Erattupetta:** **[Recommendation]** don't create a separate GBP there unless you have a fixed, staffed, signed presence with regular hours. Instead, (1) ask the hospital to list you on its own website and its GBP "Doctors / Departments" information, (2) list the Erattupetta days and timings on your website's Locations section and in GBP posts. This captures Erattupetta searches without breaking Google's rules for practitioner listings.

## B. Categories

GBP lets you choose one primary category and up to nine secondary ones. Type each term into the category box; Google's list changes, so **[Verify]** the exact wording that appears.

| Type | Recommended category | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | **Otolaryngologist** (may appear as "ENT doctor" / "Ear, nose & throat specialist") | The primary category has the most weight for "ENT doctor near me" |
| Secondary | Otolaryngology clinic | Matches clinic-type searches |
| Secondary | Doctor | Broad catch-all |
| Secondary | Medical clinic | Broad catch-all |
| Secondary | Pediatric otolaryngologist **only if Google offers it** | Paediatric ENT searches |
| Do **not** add | Audiologist, Hearing aid store, Hospital, Sleep clinic | Only add if you really offer that as a separate service (for example a qualified audiologist and audiometry booth on site) |

## E. Questions & answers (FAQ content)

Google has scaled back the public Q&A feature on some profiles. Use these as **FAQ text on your website** (with FAQ schema) and as GBP posts. If Q&A is still shown on your profile, post them there too.

1. **Do I need an appointment?** – Appointments are recommended to reduce waiting. Call or WhatsApp +91 96455 64883. Walk-ins are seen subject to availability. *(edit to match your practice)*
2. **Do you see children?** – Yes. Dr. Bibin Baby sees babies and children for ear pain, ear discharge, blocked nose, mouth breathing, snoring and tonsil problems.
3. **Is vertigo always an ear problem?** – No. Many cases of spinning vertigo come from the inner ear, but dizziness can also come from blood pressure, heart, nerve or other causes. A careful examination helps find the cause. Sudden dizziness with weakness, slurred speech, face drooping or severe headache is an emergency: go to the nearest hospital immediately.
4. **Where else can I consult Dr. Bibin Baby?** – At Sunrise Hospital, Erattupetta. Call for days and timings.
5. **Is parking available?** – Yes, patient parking is available near the Pala clinic.
6. **Which languages are spoken?** – Malayalam, English and Hindi.
7. **What should I bring?** – Previous prescriptions, hearing test or scan reports, and a list of current medicines.

## G. Ongoing GBP routine (15 minutes a week)
- 1–2 posts a week (file 5 has 30 ready).
- Reply to every new review within 48 hours (file 6).
- Check "Suggested edits" weekly. Anyone can propose changes to your hours or category, and Google sometimes applies them automatically.
- Add 2–4 new photos a month.
- Update holiday hours before every public holiday.
