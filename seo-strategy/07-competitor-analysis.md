| Opportunity | Why it's realistic | Action |

| **"Near me" Map Pack in Pala town** | A complete GBP with steady activity can compete with hospital profiles nearby | GBP completeness + photos + posts + review responses |

**Hard to win (don't chase it):** generic "ENT hospital Kottayam" against big hospitals, or "best ENT doctor in Kerala".

## D. Content comparison framework (quarterly)
For each competitor site, check: number of ENT condition pages · named doctor author on medical pages · references cited · last-updated dates · page speed (pagespeed.web.dev) · schema (Rich Results Test) · Malayalam content. Aim to beat them on **depth and trust signals for each condition**, not on the number of pages.
