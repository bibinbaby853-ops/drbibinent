# 6. Ethical Patient Feedback & Google Review Strategy

## A. Read this first: the NMC position (Oct 2026)

**[Verified, secondary sources]** NMC's *Guidelines on Ethical Advertising and Public Communication* (public notice dated 6 October 2026) bar fake, paid or misleading reviews and testimonials. Commentators report that Explanation V bars doctors from *requesting or sharing* patient testimonials, recommendations or reviews for professional promotion. Whether that covers **asking for Google reviews** is **not settled**. Several compliance advisers recommend pausing active review requests (QR codes, cards, WhatsApp) until NMC clarifies.

**[Recommendation] Use a two-tier approach:**

| Tier | Activities | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 – Always allowed** | Respond professionally to reviews patients leave on their own · collect private feedback for quality improvement · keep the GBP accurate · never post or share reviews in ads, on the website or on social media | **Start now** |
| **Tier 2 – Only after you confirm** | Neutral, non-selective "share your feedback" requests (QR, WhatsApp) | **Hold** until you have read the official NMC notice and, ideally, checked with TCMC or a lawyer |

The Tier 2 templates below are written to be as neutral as possible so they're ready if that advice allows them.

### Never, under any circumstances
- Offer discounts, free consultations, gifts or anything else for reviews.
- Ask only "happy" patients (Google calls this "review gating" and it breaks Google's rules).
- Write reviews yourself, or have staff, family or agencies write them.
- Buy reviews or use "review exchange" groups.
- Confirm in a reply that the reviewer was your patient, or mention any clinical detail.
- Reuse reviews as testimonials on the website, Instagram or posters.

## B. Tier 1: private feedback system (quality improvement, start now)

- A short **paper or Google Form feedback card** at reception: "How was your visit? Waiting time · Explanation from doctor · Staff courtesy · Cleanliness · Suggestions." This is private and **not** a Google review.
- Review the feedback monthly and fix recurring issues (waiting time is the most common complaint in local clinic reviews). Better experiences naturally produce better unsolicited reviews.

## C. Tier 2 templates (hold until confirmed permissible)

**Principles:** offer them to **every** patient equally (or to none), use neutral wording ("feedback", not "positive review"), no incentives, and make it easy to decline.

### In-clinic verbal / reception message
> "If you'd like to share feedback about your visit, the QR code at the desk opens our Google page. Whether or not you do, it makes no difference to your care."

### QR-code card / standee
- Generate the link: GBP dashboard → "Ask for reviews" → copy the link, then make a QR code in any free QR tool.
- Wording on the card:
  > **Your feedback helps us improve.**
  > Scan to share your experience of the clinic on Google.
  > *Please do not include personal medical details.*
- Placement: billing counter only, not the consultation room. No pressure at the point of care.

### WhatsApp message (send to every patient, or none, 1–2 days after the visit)
> Hello, thank you for visiting Dr. Bibin Baby – ENT Consultation, Pala. If you wish to share feedback about your visit, you can do so here: <review link>. Please avoid sharing personal health details publicly. For any concerns about your care, reply here or call +91 96455 64883. – Clinic team

## D. Responding to reviews (always allowed, and important)

**Rules:** reply within 48 hours · never confirm the person was a patient · never mention a diagnosis, procedure, visit date or result · keep it short · move complaints offline · the doctor or a designated person replies, never an agency without approval.

### Positive review templates (rotate them; don't paste the same text every time)
1. > Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback. We're glad the clinic experience met your expectations. – Dr. Bibin Baby ENT team
2. > Thank you for your kind words. Our team works to make every visit clear and comfortable, and your feedback is appreciated.
3. > We appreciate your feedback and wish you good health.
4. (Malayalam) > നിങ്ങളുടെ അഭിപ്രായം പങ്കുവെച്ചതിന് നന്ദി. നല്ല ആരോഗ്യം ആശംസിക്കുന്നു.

Avoid: "Glad your sinus surgery went well!" This confirms that the person was a patient and discloses a procedure.

### Negative review templates
**Waiting time / service complaint:**
> Thank you for your feedback, and we're sorry to hear about this experience. We are reviewing our appointment scheduling to reduce waiting times. We would value the chance to discuss this directly. Please call the clinic on +91 96455 64883.

**Unhappy with treatment or outcome (never discuss clinical details):**
> We're sorry to read this. Because of patient confidentiality we can't discuss any details here, but we take every concern seriously. Please contact the clinic on +91 96455 64883 so the doctor can speak with you directly.

**Fee / billing complaint:**
> Thank you for raising this. We aim to be transparent about consultation and procedure charges. Please contact us on +91 96455 64883 so we can look into your concern.

**Review that seems fake or from a non-patient:**
> We take all feedback seriously, but we were unable to identify this experience in our records. If you visited us, please call +91 96455 64883 so we can understand and address your concern.
*(Careful: this does not confirm or deny anyone's patient status. Separately, use "Report review" in GBP if it breaks Google's policy, for example spam, a conflict of interest or offensive content.)*

**Abusive review:** don't argue. Reply once, calmly, using the template above, and report it to Google.

## E. Monitoring
- Turn on GBP email and app notifications for new reviews.
- Track monthly: number of new reviews, average rating, response rate (target 100%), median response time (target under 48 h), and recurring themes from the text.
