## What success looks like at Day 90 (realistic, not guaranteed)
- GBP verified, 100% complete, posting weekly, with every review answered.
- No dead buttons; calls and WhatsApp clicks are tracked.
- 10 doctor-reviewed condition pages with references, all indexed.
- Your brand-name search returns your GBP and website.
- Rising impressions in GSC for the vertigo, sinus and paediatric clusters.
- A measurable baseline and trend for calls, directions and new patients citing Google.

Rankings depend on competition, distance and Google's algorithms. **No position, including first page or Map Pack top 3, can be promised.**
