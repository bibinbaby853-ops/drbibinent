### Image production tips
- Make a branded Canva template: clinic colour, logo and a small "Educational information" footer. Keep text on images under 20% of the area.
- Use the same illustration style throughout. Never use patient photos.
- File names like `gbp-post-vertigo-bppv-pala.jpg`.
