# 2. Local SEO – Keywords & Google Maps Targeting

## A. How local searching actually works here (plain language)
- Most "ENT doctor near me" and "ENT Pala" searches happen on phones and show the **Map Pack**: three GBP listings above the websites. Your GBP (file 1) decides whether you appear there. Your website supports it.
- Google mostly shows the Map Pack for clinics **near the searcher**. A person in Pala town is far more likely to see you than a person in Kottayam town, about 27 km away. **[Recommendation]** Aim to be strong in Pala, Bharananganam, Ramapuram and Erattupetta (your two locations), and build **website content** for wider Kottayam searches.
- The town is spelled both **Pala** and **Palai** (Mar Sleeva Medicity uses "Palai"). Use both naturally, for example "Pala (Palai)" once on the homepage and the contact page.

## B. Keyword volumes: how to get real numbers (free)
I did not have keyword-tool access, so **no volumes are shown**. Get real data like this:
1. **Google Search Console** (after 4–6 weeks of data): Performance → Queries. These are the searches that actually showed your site, and they are the most reliable source.
2. **Google Keyword Planner** (free with a Google Ads account, no need to spend): Tools → Keyword Planner → "Get search volume", location = *Kottayam district*. It shows ranges (for example 10–100), which is enough to prioritise.
3. **GBP Performance → "Searches breakdown"**: the actual phrases people used to find your profile.
4. **Google autocomplete and "People also ask"**: type "ENT Pala…", "vertigo Kottayam…" on a phone in Pala.

## D. Town-modifier strategy (ethical, not doorway pages)
- **Don't** create near-identical pages like "ENT doctor in Ramapuram", "ENT doctor in Bharananganam" with town names swapped. Google treats these as **doorway pages** and may demote the site.
- **Do:**
  - Create **one real page per actual consulting location** (Pala, Erattupetta) with the address, timings, a map, directions and parking.
  - Add a "Patients visit us from…" line with genuine directions: "From Bharananganam: ~5 km on the Pala–Erattupetta road…", "From Ramapuram: …", "From Kottayam: …". **[Verify]** distances.
  - Mention towns naturally in GBP posts and articles where relevant.

## E. Malayalam / Manglish searches
Many local patients search in Malayalam script or in "Manglish" (Malayalam typed in English letters). Add a short Malayalam summary block to each service page. Example phrases to **[Verify]** in autocomplete and GSC:
- ചെവി വേദന (ear pain), ചെവിയിൽ നിന്ന് പഴുപ്പ് (ear discharge), കേൾവിക്കുറവ് (hearing loss)
- തലകറക്കം (dizziness), മൂക്കടപ്പ് (nasal block), കൂർക്കംവലി (snoring), തൊണ്ടവേദന (sore throat)
- Manglish: "thalakarakkam", "chevi vedana", "mookkadappu"

## F. Claims to avoid in any keyword usage
"Best ENT doctor in Pala", "No.1", "top", "guaranteed cure", "painless", "100% success". These break NMC advertising rules and Google's healthcare ad policies. If people search "best ENT doctor in Pala", Google already treats it as "ENT doctor in Pala" with an extra weight on reviews and prominence, so you **don't need the word "best" on your pages** to appear for it.
