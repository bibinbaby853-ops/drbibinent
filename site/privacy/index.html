Privacy

Privacy and your information

How this website handles personal information, what is stored on your own device, and what reaches the doctor.

For review before publication This page describes the data flows on this website as they actually are. The legal framing — the lawful basis relied on under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Rules made under it in 2025, retention periods, and whether a consent manager is required — should be confirmed by a qualified Indian healthcare lawyer before this page goes live.

The short version

This website is a small practice website. It does not have user accounts, it does not run advertising, and it does not sell or share information with marketing companies.

  • The appointment form does not store anything on this website. It opens a message in your own WhatsApp, which you then choose to send.
  • The symptom questionnaires keep your answers in your own browser, on your own device. Nothing is transmitted automatically.
  • You decide whether to share a questionnaire result with the doctor, by using the share button yourself.

Clearing what is stored on your device

Questionnaire answers and saved reports are held in your browser's own storage for this website. You can remove them at any time:

  • Use the delete option next to a saved report inside the questionnaire tool, or
  • Clear site data for this website in your browser settings, which removes everything this site has stored.

Because this information never leaves your device, the doctor cannot retrieve it, and cannot delete it for you.

Information you send to the doctor

If you send a WhatsApp message, make a call, or share a questionnaire report, that information reaches the doctor and is handled as part of your clinical care. Clinical records are kept in line with professional record-keeping requirements.

Please note Messaging applications and ordinary telephone calls are convenient but are not a secure clinical channel. Please do not send detailed medical histories, images of a sensitive nature, or identification documents unless you have been asked to.

Your choices

  • You can use this website without completing any form or questionnaire.
  • You can ask what information the doctor holds about you, ask for a correction, or ask for information to be deleted where the law allows it.
  • You can withdraw a consent you previously gave. Withdrawal applies going forward and does not affect care already given.
  • You can raise a complaint with the doctor using the contact details below.

Children

Questionnaires about children are intended to be completed by a parent or guardian. Please do not enter a child's details unless you are their parent or guardian.

Changes to this page

If the way this website handles information changes, this page is updated and the review date below is changed.
